a source-reliability rule to keep after the course ends

three sources about a token, ranked by what they can be trusted for

the mint account on mainnet
trust it for
what is true right now — values, authorities, sizes.
fails at
intent, roadmap, and what a venue will accept.
freshness
the block you read it in.
“the law”
official docs & solutions pages
trust it for
durable facts — audit firm roster, interface descriptions, instruction semantics.
fails at
timelines and status claims — "confidential transfers expected EOY 2024" is still live.
freshness
unknown — often stale by a year or more.
“the manual, sometimes out of date”
the program repository at HEAD
trust it for
what the maintainers are building; exact processor logic.
fails at
what mainnet runs today — Token-2022 is still upgradable.
freshness
ahead of mainnet by an unknown margin.
“the future, not the present”
