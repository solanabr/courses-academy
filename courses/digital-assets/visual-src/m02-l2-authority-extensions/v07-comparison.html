PermanentDelegate
highest risk
move / burn any account of the mint
always bypasses CpiGuard; trivially sweepable
Raydium rejects it — it can sweep pool vaults
Pausable
global halt of all transfers, mints, burns
mistaken for a per-account freeze; it halts everyone
read as a centralized kill switch
DefaultAccountState (Frozen)
new accounts open frozen until thawed
confused with Pausable; it is per-account, not global
read as KYC-gated onboarding
PermissionedBurn
burns require a burn-authority co-signature
omitted from solana.com docs; the enum is truth
read as centrally-controlled supply
MintCloseAuthority
lowest risk
close a spent mint to reclaim rent
close-and-revival: a re-funded address looks fresh
mostly benign; hygiene matters
