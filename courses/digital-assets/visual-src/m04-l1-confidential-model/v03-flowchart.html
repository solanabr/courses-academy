WHY CREDITS WAIT IN A BUCKET

the life of an incoming confidential credit

1 incoming Transfer lands sender's Transfer credits your pending_balance (lo + hi ciphertexts) · counter += 1
2 the waiting room: pending_balance available_balance and the AES cache are untouched — the credit sits here until you act
the sender lacks your AES key — they cannot refresh your cache for you
guard: at maximum_pending_balance_credit_counter (default 65,536) further credits bounce until an apply runs
3 owner signs ApplyPendingBalance pending folds into available_balance homomorphically — still never decrypted on-chain
4 owner refreshes the cache supplies the new decryptable_available_balance (AES) — the wallet reads instantly again
why the bucket exists: direct credits would stale your AES cache and race your in-flight proofs
