set_transfer_fee arms a schedule — it does not fire

how a fee change actually lands, epoch by epoch

t0 · epoch N
mint initialized · older = newer = 100 bps / 5 SPROUT cap · transfers charge 100 bps
t1 · mid-epoch N — signed, not live
transfer_fee_config_authority signs set_transfer_fee(200 bps) — newer schedule armed for a coming epoch; in-flight and same-epoch transfers still charge 100 bps
100 bps in force
200 bps in force
t2 · armed epoch boundary
the newer schedule becomes effective the instant the epoch ticks over
t3 · after boundary
transfers now charge 200 bps; the 100 bps schedule is retained as older
transfers select their fee schedule by execution epoch — a signed change never applies retroactively or mid-flight.
