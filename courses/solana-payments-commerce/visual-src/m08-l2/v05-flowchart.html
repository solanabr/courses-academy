the operational cycle behind the lab files

a fair day in four scripts

1
create-pool.ts
once, at home · online
creates 4 nonce accounts on devnet, one slot per concurrent sale
→ nonce-pool.json
2
snapshot.ts
each morning · online
fetchNonce per free account; caches the live values
→ queue.json cache
offline · no connectivity
3
sign-sale.ts
at the fair · OFFLINE
per sale: signs a tx against a cached nonce, appends it + metadata
→ queue.json pending
4
drain.ts
back on signal · online
per entry: nonce still fresh? submit : reconcile/refuse
→ freed slots
next morning: back to snapshot.ts
