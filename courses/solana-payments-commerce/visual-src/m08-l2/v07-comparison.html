submit / expired / unsafe: what each bucket means

submit
rule
fresh blockhash within windowSeconds, or durable-nonce with nonceAdvanced: false
meaning at the stall
broadcast now — the order proceeds
expired
rule
blockhash entry older than windowSeconds
meaning at the stall
harmless dead bytes; re-sign against a fresh blockhash or a nonce
unsafe
rule
durable-nonce entry whose nonce already advanced
meaning at the stall
authorization spent; the runtime now rejects these bytes. The live double-charge is re-SIGNING this sale. Human decision required.
invariant
all three buckets preserve input order
