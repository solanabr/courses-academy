rent recovery on the lifecycle

when is a subscription abandoned enough to reclaim?

day 0 — pull fails
open invoice written · subscriber notified · SA live — no reclaim possible or wise
day 30 — cancelled
stated policy horizon passes OR explicit cancel arrives → mark cancelled
day 3 — reminder
settle path open the whole time
after cancel / abandon
SA closed or stale → RevokeAbandoned eligible → rent lamports return to the recorded payer
grace window — settle stays open
reclaim eligible only from here
wrong move reclaiming inside the grace window forces a returning subscriber to re-subscribe from scratch — a re-acquisition cost, not a savings
