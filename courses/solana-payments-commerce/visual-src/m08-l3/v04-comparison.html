the distinction every retry defense hangs on

rebroadcast vs re-sign

rebroadcast the same signed tx
What the chain sees
Identical bytes — the signature is the dedup key
Risk
None while the blockhash lives — the network will not process it twice
Defense needed
none
build + sign a NEW tx for the same purchase
What the chain sees
A fresh authorization, unrelated to the first
Risk
Double-fulfillment / double-charge
Defenses
  • Ledger keyed on signature
  • Reference key matched by the verifier
  • Drain routes spent nonces to unsafe
WORST COSTUMEA durable-nonce tx whose nonce already advanced is not a rebroadcast — it is a double-spend attempt with your signature on it.
