where stablecoin rails fit — and where they don't

strong fit
micro & small-ticket sales
flat sub-cent base fee vs percentage interchange
escaping settlement float
settled means spendable in seconds — no D+30, no fee to anticipate it
cross-border payments
no correspondent-bank days, near-zero fees
weak fit
high-fraud consumer verticals
buyers expect reversal on demand; finality deletes the dispute path
reversibility as the product
when the bank-backed undo is the value proposition itself
compliance-mandated reversal
flows where regulation requires an institutional reversal path
the takeaway match the rail to the flow — both columns are correct answers to different questions
