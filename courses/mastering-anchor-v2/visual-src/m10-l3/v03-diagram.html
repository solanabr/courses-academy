reload-after-CPI, and what replaced it

v1
patch it
step 1
read a typed field into a local
let before = state.total;
step 2
the CPI runs
it mutates the account data on chain
step 3
that copy is now STALE
before still holds the pre-CPI value
step 4 · the patch
.reload()? re-reads
you must remember to write it
step 5
read fresh
correct — only because of step 4
the risk: forget .reload() and you get a silent stale read — it compiles, it tests green, and it is a runtime bug.
V2
forbid it
step 1
a loaded typed account holds the data borrow
nothing can rewrite those bytes behind it
step 2 · hand it to the CPI
a typed read of THAT account = compile error
its cpi_handle_mut() borrow cannot cross the call
step 3
read after the CPI completes
the handle is consumed; the borrow is over
step 4
nothing changed underneath it
no step you can forget
the property: nothing goes stale behind a loaded account, and the one dangerous window will not compile.
v1 patches a stale copy · V2 never lets one form
