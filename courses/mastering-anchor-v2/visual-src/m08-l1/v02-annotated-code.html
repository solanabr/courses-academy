"bytemuck" tells a reader this type is not borsh. Verify your generator handles it — on either line.

{ "kind": "c" } pins the in-memory layout. Verify your generator handles it.
why it is a probe
Point your generator at an IDL carrying these two fields. Whether it reads them or silently drops them is what you actually learn.
