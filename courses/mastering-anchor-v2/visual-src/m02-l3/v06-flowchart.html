two calls bracket every cpi

The BorshAccount CPI protocol, call by call

step 1
mutate in memory
Change the deserialized value. Your edits live only in the handler’s memory.
step 2
release_borrow()
Serializes your mutations back into the buffer, then drops the guard.
step 3 · foreign code
invoke the CPI
It sees your changes — and it may take the account.
step 4
reacquire_borrow_mut()
Re-runs the owner / size / discriminator checks, then re-deserializes.
step 4 hands back
The value you read is the union of your pre-CPI mutations and the CPI’s.
step 4 rejects
A CPI that reassigned the account or swapped its discriminator.

reacquire_guard_only()
